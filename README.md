## Event Summary & FAQ

- **When & where:** Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, February 19-21 2027.
- **Qualification:** Submit a challenge at [lastc.tf](https://lastc.tf) by mid-January 2027. We will select **8 teams**.
- **Team size:** Up to 4 players per team.
- **Submissions per team:** Up to 2 challenges per team (at most 1 selected per team).
- **Travel & lodging:** Accommodation is fully covered for selected teams, plus up to $450/person travel reimbursement.
- **Category awards:** $150 each for the best challenge incorporating elements of **crypto**, **pwn**, **rev**, or **web** (max 1 category award per team). Finals prizes TBA.
- **Questions:** Join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/9cqRqX3F5g).
